# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project is a place for technical collaboration. Participants are expected to be respectful, to assume good faith, to give and accept criticism of the work without making it personal, and to keep discussion focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this standard, and may restrict participation by anyone who repeatedly does so. To report a concern, open a private security advisory on the repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and say that it concerns conduct, or contact the maintainer through the address on their GitHub profile.
